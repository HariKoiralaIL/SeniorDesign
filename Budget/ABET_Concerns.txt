Our 2D action roguelike development project faces several obstacles that could affect our development. 
First, we have financial constraints due to our limited budget and no money to hire professional artists for assets such as music. 
Sound and art on a small budget, So we have to create content for our game ourselves or rely on free/low cost resources. This limits the game's visual and audio quality. 
Security becomes a concern if we expand to multiplayer functionality. 
Ensuring player safety through secure data controls Protection against hacking or fraud And protecting player privacy must be carefully planned and executed. 
We need to take the necessary steps to avoid any security risks that may affect the player experience.
Legal restrictions also affect our design choices. Especially regarding intellectual property. 
We must ensure that our game mechanics and assets are original so other companies do not take legal action.
These constraints will shape the design of our game and push us to consider assets and other game design elements such that it can be best game we can develop.
