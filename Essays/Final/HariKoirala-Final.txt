My individual contribution to our group project focused on the user interface (UI) design. Since our project was a game, we needed a 
functional and visually appealing main menu, options screen, and other UI components. I lead an effort to prototype these elements 
using the Godot game engine. My work involved multiple iterations, as the initial designs were sometimes too cluttered, overly complex, 
or lacked visual clarity. I worked closely with my team to gather feedback and refine the UI, eventually delivering a clean and intuitive 
interface that complemented the gameplay experience. This iterative process helped ensure our final product was not only functional but 
also accessible and enjoyable for users.

Reflecting on my initial assessment from last fall, which emphasized skills in Programming and Other (like asset creation and sound design),
I primarily focused on the "Other" category, where I worked with Godot to create UI. Through this process, I learned the importance of 
simplicity and user-centered design - understanding that a good UI helps players easily navigate the game without frustration. I also 
strengthened my ability to take feedback and adapt designs accordingly. One of my biggest successes was turning rough prototypes into a 
polished, cohesive UI that matched our game’s theme. A key obstacle was balancing functionality with aesthetics, but this challenge helped
me grow both creatively and technically.
